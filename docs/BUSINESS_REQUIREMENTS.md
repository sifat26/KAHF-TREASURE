# BUSINESS_REQUIREMENTS.md

> **Document Purpose**
>
> This document defines the business goals, user expectations, required website functionality, and future roadmap for the KAHF Treasure website.
>
> It does not describe implementation details or UI components.
>
> All design and development decisions should support these business requirements.

---

# Project Information

## Business Name

KAHF Treasure

## Business Type

Premium Alcohol-Free Attar & Islamic Lifestyle Brand

## Industry

Fragrance / Attar / Islamic Lifestyle Products

## Primary Market

Bangladesh

## Business Model

Direct-to-Customer (D2C)

Customers browse products online and place orders primarily through WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, or direct contact.

Currently there is **no online payment gateway** and **no checkout system**.

---

# Business Goals

The website should help the business achieve the following goals.

## Primary Goals

- Build trust with new customers.
- Present KAHF Treasure as a premium brand.
- Increase WhatsApp orders.
- Make browsing products simple.
- Increase customer engagement.
- Improve conversion rate.
- Strengthen online brand presence.

---

## Secondary Goals

- Improve search engine visibility (SEO).
- Encourage repeat customers.
- Make future expansion easier.
- Support future e-commerce functionality.
- Reduce customer confusion during ordering.

---

# Target Audience

The website is primarily designed for:

- Customers in Bangladesh.
- People interested in premium alcohol-free attar.
- Islamic lifestyle customers.
- Gift buyers.
- Customers looking for long-lasting fragrances.
- Mobile-first users.
- Social-media-driven shoppers.

# Customer Journey

A visitor should naturally move through this flow.

1. Discover the brand.
2. Understand why KAHF Treasure is different.
3. Browse collections.
4. Explore products.
5. Compare options.
6. Build confidence.
7. Contact the business.
8. Place an order.

Every page should reduce friction during this journey.

# Business Constraints

The redesign must:

- Preserve all existing product information.
- Preserve existing contact channels.
- Maintain a premium brand identity.
- Remain fast and responsive.
- Be easy to maintain.
- Support future business growth.
- Allow future e-commerce expansion without major redesign.

---

# Final Business Vision

KAHF Treasure should be recognized as one of Bangladesh's most trusted premium alcohol-free attar brands.

The website should not feel like a simple product catalog. It should represent a complete luxury fragrance brand that builds trust, creates desire, and encourages customers to place orders while remaining scalable for future business growth.
